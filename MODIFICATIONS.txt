Pocket adaptation changes, prepared 18–20 October 2026
Public source snapshot published 22 October 2026

Test.5 changes prepared 24–25 October 2026 (Pocket feedback received 25 October):
- Loading raster with rendered title screenshot, real drained-byte percentage,
  frame-coherent progress display and switch to the game at vertical sync.
- Deferred ROM reads increased from 1 KiB to 4 KiB, preserving bounded buffering
  and physical-write drain before the next request or game start.
- Pocket yunit_mem_sameclock removes one redundant cooldown CE interval between
  consecutive CPU memory transactions; other users retain the legacy default.
- Aligned 16/32-bit RAM stores skip unnecessary old-word reads. Partial fields
  retain read-modify-write, bounds checks and physical-write acknowledgments.
- Pocket program reads use a synchronous direct-mapped cache of 64 16-bit words,
  filled from completed reads and invalidated by loader writes. The original
  single-word cache remains the default for other hosts.
- Pocket dense 8-bpp graphics prefetch uses aligned bursts of up to four 64-bit
  beats in the existing cache slots. The owner drains every response; CPU,
  demand and download transactions remain single-beat. Other hosts keep the
  original single-beat prefetch default.
- No change to CPU clock enable rate, pixel/audio/memory clocks, DMA cadence,
  publication fences or game ROMs.
- Synthetic RTL regressions cover these paths and loading-image reproduction.
  Component timing gains do not establish whole-game performance. The exact
  compiled image passed four-corner timing and memory-interface review.
  The Pocket tester confirmed smoother gameplay and the loading display.
  No quantitative arcade-speed or frame-rate claim is made.
